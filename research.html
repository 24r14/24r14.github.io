02 / Research

Questions before
conclusions.

I’m interested in how we move from an observation about people to a question we can investigate carefully.

Research interests & exploratory questions
01

Learning & attention

What helps a useful idea become an action?

Collecting information is only one part of learning. I’m curious about what happens between understanding an idea, remembering it, and using it in a real situation.

Connected to my personal manual and learning notes.

02

Decision making

How does context shape a choice?

I’m interested in the relationship between intentions, habits, and the environment—and in how changing a situation might change what people do.

A question at the intersection of psychology and everyday design.

03

Culture & human behavior

How do cultural ideas shape experience?

I want to explore how people interpret traditions and explanations of human experience, and how to approach those questions with clear definitions and careful evidence.

An exploratory interest, with questions still taking shape.

How I want
to investigate.

Start with a specific question. Separate an appealing explanation from the evidence behind it. Ask what was measured, who took part, and what other explanations remain.

These are directions I want to explore, rather than a list of completed studies or publications.
