01 / Ball State University · 2026–present

Psychology of Space & Time Lab

Student Research Assistant · Dr. Daniele Nardi

My work

02 / Independent writing · Summer 2026

Recognition and overdiagnosis

Psychology essay · John Locke Essay Competition

Essay completed

I reviewed psychology and public health literature and wrote a 15-page essay examining improved recognition versus overdiagnosis of adolescent mental health conditions.

The question

How can we examine the distinction between recognizing needs more accurately and expanding diagnostic labels too far?

The work

Literature review and sustained academic writing, with attention to competing interpretations and the evidence behind them.

03 / Research Science coursework

A political science research question

In development

Through Research Science coursework, I am developing a political science research question and working toward a possible poster or panel proposal.

The question and project are still taking shape. This is ongoing coursework.

What I bring
to a paper.

A short account of the question, the method, the finding, and the limitation helps me see what a source can support. It also helps me separate a study’s result from a broader interpretation.

My interests include cognitive and clinical psychology, learning, and connections between culture and human experience.
