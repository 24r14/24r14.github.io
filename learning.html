A framework
for reading.

Rather than saving every passage, I want to leave a reading session with a clear question and a short account of what the source actually supports.

  1. QuestionWhat is the author trying to explain?
  2. EvidenceWhat was observed or measured?
  3. LimitsWhat does this source leave uncertain?
  4. ConnectionWhere does it meet another idea or experience?
  5. Next stepWhat can I investigate, explain, or try?

A personal experiment

After the
screenshot.

A screenshot can preserve something that caught my attention without preserving why it mattered. As I sort through saved posts, I am developing a way to turn a few of them into useful notes.

  1. Recover the contextKeep the author, original link, and date when available. Write down what caught my attention.
  2. Separate the piecesDistinguish a factual claim, an interpretation, a personal story, and a suggestion to try.
  3. Choose a destinationKeep it as a reference, turn it into a question, or choose a small action to test. Let the rest go.
  4. Return to itRecord what I learned or changed. A saved idea can stay an open question.
