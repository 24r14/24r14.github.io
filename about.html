01 / About

Following questions.
Making connections.

I’m Zhuodan Yin. I’m a student interested in psychology, research, and the space between understanding people and making something useful.

The thread
through my interests.

My interests often start with an everyday question: Why do people make a particular choice? What helps an idea stay with someone? How can a project make it easier for people to take part?

Psychology gives me a way to ask these questions more carefully. Community projects give me a reason to see what happens outside the page. Building and writing help me make the connections visible.

This website brings those threads together. It is a record of work in progress, things I have tried, and questions I want to keep exploring.
