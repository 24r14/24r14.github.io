01 / Community · 2025–2026

Our Closet

Muncie Youth Leadership Program

Completed team project

A student-led collection project supporting an elementary school with donated clothing, school supplies, and everyday essentials.

The purpose

Bring a community collection effort into a form that people could understand, contribute to, and help carry through.

My contribution

I helped organize collection at my school, worked on poster design and social media, sorted donations, and reached out to potential sources of support.

304school supplies collected
100take-home bags collected

Team outcomes documented in the existing project portfolio. Material counts do not represent a count of students served.

More about the process & evidence +

My work included reaching out to Cardinal Closet and following up with another school when the first school had no room for further donations. Outreach is distinct from a confirmed partnership or a completed additional delivery.

The project portfolio includes collection records, poster designs, planning materials, the final presentation, and program feedback. The presentation award belongs to the team.

02 / Learning · Reflection

A personal manual

Ongoing exploration

A way to turn useful ideas from reading and everyday experience into a small collection of principles, defaults, and experiments.

The question

How can I keep good ideas in a form that I will actually use, instead of building a collection I never revisit?

The approach

Distinguish reference material from active practices. Give each experiment a clear situation, a small action, and a point for review.

Still open: which ideas remain useful after trying them, and which should be revised or set aside?
