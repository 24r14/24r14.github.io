01 / Community · 2025–2026

Our Closet

Muncie Youth Leadership Program

Completed team project

A student-led collection project supporting two elementary schools with donated clothing, school supplies, and everyday essentials.

The purpose

Bring a community collection effort into a form that people could understand, contribute to, and help carry through.

My contribution

As part of a five-member cross-school team, I organized donation collection at my school, designed posters, helped run social media, sorted donations, and contacted community resources and local schools.

304school supplies collected
100take-home bags collected
Winning projectMuncie Youth Leadership Program

Team outcomes: 14 bags of clothing, 8 totes of other essentials, 304 school supplies, and 100 take-home bags for two elementary schools. Material counts describe collected items.

More about the process & evidence +

I personally contacted Ball State’s Cardinal Closet and local schools to explore donations and distribution support. Our five-member team presented the completed project together.

The project portfolio includes poster designs, planning materials, collection records, the final presentation, and program feedback. Our Closet was recognized as the program’s winning project, a shared team achievement.

05 / Learning · Reflection

A personal manual

Ongoing exploration

An ongoing effort to turn saved reading, screenshots, and everyday observations into notes I can return to: questions, working principles, and small experiments.

The question

After saving a post, how do I decide what it means, what needs checking, and whether it changes anything I do?

The approach

I am developing a review process: recover the source, put the idea into my own words, and choose whether to keep it as a reference, investigate a question, or try a small action with a review point.

Still open: which ideas remain useful after trying them, and which should be revised or set aside?
